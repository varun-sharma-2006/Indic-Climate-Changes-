The problem

Labelled climate-stance data exists only in English, but we need to classify Hindi and Bengali comments. Our base paper, Luo, Card & Jurafsky (2020), “Detecting Stance in Media on Global Warming”, solved this for English news only. Its model (English BERT) gets 75% accuracy on English but fails on Hindi/Bengali.

How we trained the model

1 · English dataGWSD (base paper, 2,050 news sentences) + ClimateStance (3,580 tweets)
→
2 · TranslateNLLB-200 → Hindi and Bengali (same labels), plus romanised copies
→
3 · CleanURLs, @mentions, hashtags, emojis → words, repeated letters
→
4 · Fine-tuneMuRIL (Indian-language BERT) on En + Hi + Bn, class-weighted loss
→
5 · EvaluateMacro F1 on 555 test sentences per language
